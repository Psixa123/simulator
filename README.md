# ⛈️ Storm Catcher Simulator

A Roblox simulator where you play a storm chaser with a weather vacuum. Suck up clouds, tornadoes and
solar flares, sell the energy for **Volts**, upgrade your gear, unlock wilder biomes and **Climate Shift**
for permanent bonuses and new gear looks.

The whole game is code: the map, the models, the interface and the sounds are generated by Luau scripts
that [Rojo](https://rojo.space) syncs into Roblox Studio. This README assumes you're on **Windows** and
have never used Rojo before.

## Features

### The core loop

1. **Catch** – equip the Storm Vacuum and hold click (or hold your finger on the screen on mobile) to
   vacuum the weather under your cursor or the nearest one in range. The energy goes into your Storm
   Tank backpack, which glows as it fills up.
2. **Sell** – stand on a gold sell pad (next to the Weather Lab in every biome and in the hub) to turn
   your energy into Volts. The better the biome and the rarer the weather, the more each tank is worth.
3. **Upgrade** – at the Upgrade Workshop (or the ⬆️ Upgrades menu):
   - 💨 **Vacuum Power** – drain weather faster; each biome needs a minimum Power to enter.
   - 🛢️ **Storm Tank** – carry more energy before you need to sell.
   - 📡 **Vacuum Range** – reach weather from further away.
   - 👟 **Storm Boots** – run faster (and stay warm in Frostpeak).
4. **Unlock biomes** – pay Volts at the gate (or in the 🗺️ Teleport menu) once your Vacuum Power is high
   enough. Unlocked biomes can be teleported to at any time.
5. **Climate Shift** – trade your Volts, Vacuum Power and Storm Tank levels for a permanent Volts bonus
   and new gear looks (see below), then do it all again faster.

A guide beam and an objective tracker walk new players through every step.

### Six biomes

| # | Biome | Unlock cost | Power needed | Hazard |
|---|-------|-------------|--------------|--------|
| 1 | 🌤️ Sunny Plains | free | – | None, it's the safe starter biome. |
| 2 | ⛈️ Thunder Valley | 5K Volts | 28 | **Lightning strikes**: a glowing circle warns you, then the bolt **stuns** everyone inside it for a moment. Keep moving! |
| 3 | 🏜️ Dune Sea | 800K Volts | 120 | **Sandstorms** sweep in every so often and bury the view in dust (**low visibility**). |
| 4 | 🏔️ Frostpeak | 60M Volts | 520 | **Freezing cold slows you down** unless your **Storm Boots** are upgraded enough (level 9 by default). |
| 5 | 🌋 Ashfall Volcano | 4B Volts | 2,400 | **Scorching heat** deals damage over time. Cool off (and heal) in the **cooling zones** around the sell pad / Weather Lab, the egg station and the biome's spawn. |
| 6 | 🌌 Upper Atmosphere | 300B Volts | 11,000 | **Low gravity** for floaty jumps, among solar flares and auroras. |

Hazards never apply in the hub (Storm Station) or the Mega Storm arena. Each biome has three kinds of
weather (common, uncommon and a **rare** one that sparkles and is worth much more), its own egg, lighting,
ambient particles and sounds. Costs, Power requirements and hazard settings live in
`src/shared/Config/Zones.luau`.

### 🐾 Stormlings (pets)

- Hatch Stormlings from the **egg station in each biome** (single hatch, or Triple/Auto Hatch with
  gamepasses). Rarities go from Common to Legendary, plus **Exclusive** pets that only drop from Mega Storms.
- Equipped Stormlings follow you, **boost your energy multiplier** and **catch weather near you
  automatically**.
- Combine **5 of the same pet into a Golden** one, and **5 Golden into a Rainbow** one for much bigger
  bonuses.
- 3 pets can be equipped at once (6 with the +3 Pet Slots pass); storage holds 60.
- A Pet Index tracks every Stormling you've discovered.

### 🌪️ Mega Storms

Every **~10 minutes** (the first one 3 minutes after a server starts) a giant storm appears in the hub
arena after a 30-second warning. Everyone vacuums it together for 2 minutes. Rewards scale with how
much you drained, and the **top 3 contributors** get the best chance at an Exclusive pet
(25% / 15% / 10%), with a small chance for everyone else who helped. There are five storm types
(Tempest Twister, Thunder Titan, Blizzard Behemoth, Inferno Cyclone, Cosmic Maelstrom). Players can also
buy a **Summon Mega Storm** product that calls one for the whole server.

### 🌈 Rainbow Hurricane and the Forecast Board

A rare **Rainbow Hurricane** strikes **every 2 hours, on the hour, UTC** (00:00, 02:00, 04:00, …), at the same
time in every server, with a server-wide heads-up a few minutes before it arrives. It is tougher and pays
out more than a normal Mega Storm. The **Forecast Board** in the hub shows what's coming next, for example
*"Rainbow Hurricane in 2h 14m"*, so players know when to come back. Tune it in
`GameConfig.MegaStorm.Rainbow`.

### 🛰️ Storm Radar

Owners of the **Storm Radar** gamepass get an alert whenever rare weather spawns in any biome they've
unlocked, with a marker that leads them to it.

### 🌀 Climate Shift (rebirth)

Climate Shifting costs 10M Volts, and the price grows 5x with every shift. You lose your Volts, Vacuum Power
and Storm Tank levels, but you keep your pets, biomes, Range, Storm Boots, gamepasses and boosts, and
you get **+50% Volts permanently** for every shift. Shifts also unlock **gear looks**: new colours, glow and
(later) a trail for your Storm Tank and Storm Vacuum. The best look you've unlocked is worn automatically.

| Climate Shifts | Look |
|---------------:|------|
| 0 | ⚙️ Storm Standard |
| 1 | 🌱 Spring Shower |
| 2 | ⚡ Thunderstruck |
| 3 | 🏜️ Desert Mirage |
| 5 | ❄️ Polar Vortex (adds a trail) |
| 8 | 🌋 Molten Core |
| 12 | 🌌 Aurora Borealis |
| 20 | 🌈 Rainbow Tempest (rainbow trail) |

The Climate Shift panel (🌀 menu button or the Climate Shrine in the hub) shows the look you're wearing
and the next one to unlock. Looks are defined in `GameConfig.Rebirth.Cosmetics`. Internally the system
is still called "Rebirth" (`RebirthService`, `data.Rebirths`), so old saves keep working.

### 🎁 Rewards and codes

- **Daily rewards** – a 7-day login streak (claim every 20 hours; the streak restarts if you miss 48 hours).
  Day 7 gives the Sunbeam pet.
- **Session gifts** – free gifts after 2, 5, 10, 15, 25, 40, 60 and 90 minutes of playing in a server.
- **Codes** (not case-sensitive, once per player), defined in `src/shared/Config/Rewards.luau`:

  | Code | Reward |
  |------|--------|
  | `RELEASE` | Volts (5 tanks' worth) |
  | `STORMY` | 2x Energy boost for 15 minutes |
  | `LUCKY` | 2x Luck boost for 15 minutes |
  | `THUNDER` | Volts (10 tanks' worth) |

### Everything else

- Global leaderboards in the hub: 🏆 Top Storm Catchers (total Volts) and 🌀 Most Climate Shifts.
- Timed 2x Energy / 2x Luck boosts that pause while you're offline.
- Settings: music, sound effects, other players' pets, Performance Mode (fewer particles) and Auto Catch.
- Data saving with session locking, autosave and receipt tracking so purchases are never lost or
  granted twice.
- The interface scales for phones, tablets and PCs.

## First-time setup

### Option A: Aftman (recommended)

[Aftman](https://github.com/LPGhatguy/aftman) installs the exact Rojo version this project pins in
`aftman.toml` (`rojo-rbx/rojo@7.7.0`).

1. Download the latest `aftman-…-windows-x86_64.zip` from
   <https://github.com/LPGhatguy/aftman/releases>, unzip it, open a terminal (PowerShell) in that folder
   and run:
   ```powershell
   .\aftman.exe self-install
   ```
2. **Close and reopen** your terminal so the new PATH is picked up.
3. Go to the project folder and install Rojo:
   ```powershell
   cd C:\dev\simulator
   aftman install
   ```
   The first time, Aftman asks whether you trust `rojo-rbx/rojo`. Answer **y**.
4. Check it worked:
   ```powershell
   rojo --version
   ```
   It should print `Rojo 7.7.0`.
5. Install the Studio plugin (restart Studio afterwards if it was open):
   ```powershell
   rojo plugin install
   ```

([Rokit](https://github.com/rojo-rbx/rokit), Aftman's successor, also reads `aftman.toml` if you
prefer it.)

### Option B: the Rojo VS Code extension

1. In VS Code, open the Extensions tab and install **"Rojo - Roblox Studio Sync"**.
2. Open the project folder in VS Code. The extension offers to install Rojo and the Studio plugin;
   accept both. Pick version 7.7 if it asks.
3. Use the extension's **Rojo** panel to start/stop the server instead of typing `rojo serve`.

## Syncing code into Studio

1. Build a fresh place file once (see [Building a place file](#building-a-place-file)) and open it in
   Studio by double-clicking `StormCatcher.rbxl`.
2. In a terminal in the project folder, start the Rojo server:
   ```powershell
   rojo serve
   ```
   It listens on `localhost:34872` and uses `default.project.json`.
3. In Studio, open the **Plugins** tab → **Rojo** → **Connect**.
4. Press **Play** (F5). The server builds the whole map when the game starts, so the world looks
   empty in Edit mode. That's normal.

From now on, every time you save a `.luau` file it shows up in Studio right away.
**Edit scripts in your code editor, not inside Studio**: Rojo doesn't write Studio edits back to
the files, and the next sync overwrites them.

## Building a place file

To get a complete `.rbxl` you can open, share or publish:

```powershell
rojo build -o StormCatcher.rbxl
```

This uses `default.project.json`. Place files in a `build/` folder are ignored by git, so this keeps
your working copy clean (Rojo doesn't create the folder for you):

```powershell
mkdir build
rojo build -o build\StormCatcher.rbxl
```

## Publishing the game

1. Open the place in Studio (built file or synced, either works).
2. **File → Publish to Roblox As…** → create a new experience (for example "Storm Catcher Simulator").
3. **Home → Game Settings → Security** → turn on **Enable Studio Access to API Services** → **Save**.
   This lets you test saving and leaderboards (DataStores) inside Studio. Live servers always have
   DataStores.
4. Set the icon and thumbnails. On the Creator Hub (<https://create.roblox.com/dashboard/creations>)
   open your experience → **Configure**:
   - **Icon**: `assets/icon.png` (512×512) or `assets/icon_1024.png`
   - **Thumbnails**: every file in `assets/thumbnails/` (1920×1080)
5. Create the gamepasses and products and paste their IDs into the code
   ([next section](#gamepasses-and-developer-products)), and do the same for the
   [sounds](#sounds-and-music).
6. Publish again (**File → Publish to Roblox**, Alt+P) after changing IDs.
7. When you're ready, make the experience **Public** in the Creator Hub (you'll need to fill in the
   maturity questionnaire first).

starts at **0**.

### Creating them on the Creator Hub

You have to publish the game first (passes and products belong to an experience).

**Gamepasses**
1. Go to <https://create.roblox.com/dashboard/creations>, open your experience.
2. **Monetization → Passes → Create a Pass**. Upload the icon from the table, enter the name and a
   description (you can copy the `Description` from `Monetization.luau`), then **Create Pass**.
3. Open the new pass → **Sales** → turn on **Item for Sale**, set the price from the table, save.
4. Copy the pass ID (the number in the page URL, or the **⋯ → Copy Asset ID** menu on the pass).
5. Paste it into `Monetization.luau`, for example:
   ```lua
   VIP = {
       Id = 123456789, -- was 0
   ```

**Developer products**
1. Same experience → **Monetization → Developer Products → Create a Developer Product**.
2. Upload the icon, set the name and price from the table, save.
3. Copy the product ID and paste it into the matching entry under `Monetization.Products`.

Save the file (Rojo syncs it), then publish the game again.

## Sounds and music

The sound files are in `assets/audio`. Until they're uploaded, the game uses a few built-in Roblox
sounds or plays nothing.

1. Upload each `.wav`: Creator Hub → **Creations → Development Items → Audio → Upload Asset**, or in
   Studio open the **Asset Manager** (View tab) → **Bulk Import**. Upload with the **same account or group that owns
   the experience**, otherwise Roblox won't let the game play them.
2. Wait for each upload to be approved, copy its asset ID and paste it into
   `src/shared/Config/Sounds.luau`:

| Key in `Sounds.luau` | File |
|----------------------|------|
| `Click` | `click.wav` |
| `Error` | `error.wav` |
| `Sell` | `sell.wav` |
| `Success` | `success.wav` |
| `Hatch` | `hatch.wav` |
| `Pop` | `pop.wav` |
| `Alarm` | `alarm.wav` |
| `Vacuum` | `vacuum_loop.wav` |
| `Music` | `music_loop.wav` |

(`Thunder` has no file; it uses a built-in Roblox sound.) While an `Id` is 0 the `Fallback` sound is used,
or the sound is skipped.

## Running the smoke test

`test.project.json` is the same game plus a test harness (`tests/TestHarness.server.luau` and
`tests/TestHarness.client.luau`). The harness only runs inside Studio and plays through the game like a
player would:

- checks that the world was built (map parts, weather cells, gates) and that you spawned in the hub with
  your vacuum, tank, overhead tag and leaderstats
- catches weather and sells it at a pad
- buys upgrades, opens every panel and tab
- claims the daily reward, redeems a code, rejects a fake code, changes a setting
- hatches eggs, gives pets, equips the best, crafts Golden (and Rainbow) pets
- unlocks and teleports to every biome and catches weather in each one
- tries the biome hazards
- does a Climate Shift and checks that your gear switched to the new look
- summons a Mega Storm, vacuums it until it ends
- shows notifications back in the hub

To run it:

```powershell
rojo build test.project.json -o StormCatcher_Test.rbxl
```

Open `StormCatcher_Test.rbxl` in Studio, press **Play** (F5) and watch the **Output** window. Every check
prints `[HARNESS-C] PASS …` or a yellow `[HARNESS-C] FAIL …` warning, and the run ends with
`[HARNESS-C] SUMMARY passed=… failed=…` (it takes a few minutes because it waits for the Mega Storm).
The harness also throws one *"HARNESS deliberate error (ignore)"* on purpose, and prints `[SHOT] …` lines
that an external screenshot tool can use.

The harness only runs in an **unpublished** place (one opened from the `.rbxl` file, so
`game.PlaceId` is 0). That's deliberate: it grants huge amounts of Volts, unlocks every biome and
does a Climate Shift, which must never be saved to real player data or your live leaderboards.
So don't `rojo serve test.project.json` into your published place; always use the built test file.

> ⚠️ Never publish the test build: it contains a debug remote that gives Volts and pets.

## Artwork and audio tools

### Re-rendering the artwork

The game icon, thumbnails and gamepass/product icons are HTML + SVG files in `assets/source`, rendered to
PNG by headless Chrome or Edge:

```powershell
python tools/render_art.py          # everything
python tools/render_art.py icon     # only outputs whose name contains "icon"
```

Requirements: Python 3 and **Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge** installed in the usual
`C:\Program Files…` location (on macOS/Linux it also finds Chrome or Chromium; set the
`CHROME_PATH` environment variable to point at any other Chromium-based browser). Output goes to
`assets/icon.png`, `assets/icon_1024.png`, `assets/thumbnails/`, `assets/gamepasses/` and
`assets/products/`. The sources are plain HTML/SVG pages in `assets/source/` (`icon.html`,
`thumbnail1-3.html`, and `badge.html` for every gamepass and product icon).

### Regenerating the audio

```powershell
python tools/generate_audio.py
```

It synthesizes every sound effect and the music loop from scratch with Python 3's standard library (no
extra packages) and overwrites the files in `assets/audio`. Upload them again afterwards and update the
IDs in `Sounds.luau`.

## Project structure

```
simulator/
├── default.project.json   Rojo project: which folder goes where in the game
├── test.project.json      Same game + the smoke test harness
├── aftman.toml            Pins Rojo 7.7.0
├── src/
│   ├── shared/            → ReplicatedStorage.Shared (used by server and client)
│   │   ├── Config/        All tuning: GameConfig, Zones, Weather, Pets, Rewards,
│   │   │                  Monetization, Sounds, MapLayout
│   │   ├── Models/        Builds pets, eggs and weather out of parts
│   │   ├── Util/          Number/time formatting, a tiny Signal class
│   │   ├── Formulas.luau  Game math shared by server and client
│   │   ├── ClimateShift.luau  Which gear look your Climate Shifts unlock
│   │   └── Remotes.luau   Typed access to the RemoteEvents/RemoteFunctions
│   ├── server/            → ServerScriptService.Server
│   │   ├── Main.server.luau   Loads every service in ORDER, then Init() and Start()
│   │   └── Services/      DataService, WeatherService, PetService, MegaStormService,
│   │                      MapService (builds the world), PlayerService, CharacterGear, …
│   └── client/            → StarterPlayer.StarterPlayerScripts.Client
│       ├── Main.client.luau   Loads every controller, builds the panels
│       ├── Controllers/   HUD, effects, vacuum input, pets, zones, sounds, Mega Storm, …
│       └── UI/            UI kit (UI.luau, Theme.luau), Panels/ and Overlays/
├── tests/                 Smoke test (only in test.project.json)
├── assets/                Icon, thumbnails, pass/product icons, audio + their sources
└── tools/                 render_art.py, generate_audio.py
```

A few rules if you add code:
- A new **server service** goes in `src/server/Services/` and must be added to `ORDER` in
  `Main.server.luau`; a new **client controller** goes in `src/client/Controllers/` and into `ORDER` in
  `Main.client.luau` (panels go into its `PANELS` list).
- A new **remote** must be added to `default.project.json`, `test.project.json` **and**
  `src/shared/Remotes.luau`.
- New saved data goes into `template()` in `DataService.luau`; old saves get the new keys
  automatically. Never rename existing keys, or players lose that progress.

## Tweaking the balance

Almost every number lives in `src/shared/Config/`:

| File | What's in it |
|------|--------------|
| `GameConfig.luau` | Catch speed, upgrade value/cost curves and max levels, Climate Shift cost, bonus and gear looks (`Rebirth`), pet slots and Golden/Rainbow rules, gamepass effects, boosts, Mega Storm timing and rewards (incl. the Rainbow Hurricane), default settings, DataStore name |
| `Zones.luau` | Biome unlock cost, required Power, Volts per energy, weather health, which weather/egg each biome has, lighting, hazards |
| `Weather.luau` | Weather types and rarities (health/value multipliers, spawn chances) |
| `Pets.luau` | Every Stormling, its rarity and bonus, eggs, egg prices and hatch chances |
| `Rewards.luau` | Daily streak rewards, session gifts, promo codes |
| `Monetization.luau` | Gamepasses and developer products (IDs, prices, descriptions) |
| `Sounds.luau` | Sound IDs and volumes |
| `MapLayout.luau` | Where everything is placed in the world |

The formulas that combine them (multipliers, tank capacity, Climate Shift cost, rewards) are in
`src/shared/Formulas.luau`. Changing `GameConfig.DataStoreName` starts everyone from scratch, so only do
that on purpose.

## Troubleshooting

**"[DataService] Using temporary data because DataStores are unavailable" / leaderboards say
"Leaderboard unavailable" in Studio.**
Studio can't use DataStores until the game is published and **Game Settings → Security → Enable Studio
Access to API Services** is on. Until then you play with temporary data that isn't saved.

**`rojo serve` fails with "address already in use" (port 34872).**
Another Rojo server is still running (another terminal, or the VS Code extension). Close it, or use a
different port and type the same port into the Studio plugin:
```powershell
rojo serve --port 34873
```

**The Rojo plugin won't connect or complains about versions.**
Make sure `rojo serve` is running and that the plugin is 7.x like the CLI (`rojo plugin install`
installs the matching one).

**`aftman` or `rojo` is "not recognized".**
Close and reopen the terminal after `aftman self-install`. If it still fails, sign out of Windows and
back in so the PATH change applies.

**Weird git errors, files reverting, or Rojo missing changes, with the project in OneDrive.**
OneDrive syncs files while git and Rojo are using them, which can corrupt the `.git` folder and confuse
Rojo's file watcher. Move the project to a folder OneDrive doesn't sync, for example `C:\dev\simulator`.

**The world is empty in Edit mode.**
That's expected. `MapService` builds the map when the server starts. Press Play.

**Buying something in the live game says "coming soon".**
That item's `Id` is still 0 in `Monetization.luau`. See
[Gamepasses and developer products](#gamepasses-and-developer-products).

**No sounds or music.**
The `Id`s in `Sounds.luau` are still 0, the upload wasn't approved yet, or the audio was uploaded by a
different account/group than the one that owns the experience.

**I edited a script in Studio and my change disappeared.**
Rojo overwrites Studio's copy with the files on disk. Make your changes in the `.luau` files.
